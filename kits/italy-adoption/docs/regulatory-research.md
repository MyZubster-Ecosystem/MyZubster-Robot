# Italy / EU Regulatory Research (issue #24)

Per-regulator crypto/RWA + agri-tech landscape for the Italian home-base market. Static snapshot; operators must verify with live sources and legal counsel before operating.

## Banca d'Italia (BDI)
- **Framework**: Primary AML/prudential supervisor for VASPs (virtual-asset service providers); 2020 ban on crypto custody by banks was overturned in 2022; MiCA-aligned VASP regime being phased in
- **Monero**: No specific privacy-coin ban; subject to general AML/CFT enhanced-diligence for transfers between unhosted wallets
- **RWA**: Asset-referencing tokens fall partly under MiCA (effective end-2024); bank-issued tokens under banking supervision
- **Readiness**: 3/5 (framework in flux during MiCA transition)
- **Actions**: Register/authorize as VASP with OAM (Organismo Agenti e Mediatori) registry; appoint AML officer; implement Travel Rule (TFR) for >=1,000 EUR transfers

## Consob
- **Framework**: Securities regulator; prospectus requirements; MiCA for crypto-asset issuance and CASP (crypto-asset service provider) authorization
- **Monero**: Not a security per se but secondary-market trading of asset-referencing tokens is MiCA-scoped
- **RWA**: Tokenized securities subject to CONSOB prospectus/secondary-market rules; "DL capitali" (private capital law) frames digital-bond issuance
- **Readiness**: 3/5 (MiCA convergence ongoing; case-by-case token classification)
- **Actions**: Obtain MiCA CASP authorization for custody/exchange; comply with prospectus tests if issuing security tokens; use sandbox (RegTech) pilots for first issuance

## Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (GDPR)
- **Framework**: EU GDPR enforcer; lawful basis (Art.6) + data-minimization for on-farm sensor data and beta-tester profiles; 2019 guidance flagged pseudonymization as still personal data
- **Monero**: Wallet addresses may be personal data when linked to identifiers; privacy coins do not exempt from GDPR
- **RWA**: Sensor/telemetry streams from robots are personal data if re-identifiable; field-trial footage needs consent
- **Readiness**: 4/5 (clear, mature framework)
- **Actions**: Collect Art.6 lawful basis (consent/legitimate-interest DPIA) for beta testers; minimize on-farm telemetry; define retention + data-subject procedures

## MIPAAF (Ministero delle politiche agricole alimentari e forestali)
- **Framework**: Agricultural ministry; field-trial authorizations; PSR/RDP (Rural Development Programmes) co-funding for agri-tech pilots; SA الاجراء-issue of drones/robots on farm land
- **Monero**: Indirect (agri-to-farmer payment flows); no direct crypto remit
- **RWA**: Tokenized cooperative shares / grain receipts (titoli) intersect with agri-cooperative law
- **Readiness**: 4/5 (established field-trial framework)
- **Actions**: File field-trial protocol for Castrocaro pilot; apply for PSR co-funding; document crop-safety and operator-training plan

## Overall regulatory readiness

Aggregate score (mean of 4 regulators): ~70/100 -> passes the 60-point entry gate. Italy is the home base with the strongest synergy; the open items are MiCA-transition (BDI/Consob) which stabilize through 2024-2026.
