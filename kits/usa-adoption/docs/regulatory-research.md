# US Regulatory Research (issue #29)

Per-regulator crypto/RWA landscape for the US market. Static snapshot; operators must verify with live sources and legal counsel before operating.

## SEC (U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission)
- **Framework**: Howey Test for investment contracts; digital assets evaluated case-by-case under federal securities law
- **Monero**: Privacy coins not specifically banned; subject to general securities registration if offered as an investment contract
- **RWA Tokenization**: Real-world-asset tokens risk classification as securities if they represent profit-sharing from a common enterprise
- **Readiness**: 3/5 (moderate clarity -- case-by-case approach creates uncertainty)
- **Actions**: Register if issuing security tokens; obtain Reg D exemption for private placement; ensure accredited-investor constraints where required

## CFTC (Commodity Futures Trading Commission)
- **Framework**: Classifies BTC and ETH as commodities under CEA (Commodity Exchange Act); Monero is commodity-adjacent
- **Monero**: Not directly classified but commodity-adjacent; derivatives regulated by CFTC
- **RWA**: Physical commodity-backed tokens may fall under CFTC jurisdiction if fungible and deliverable
- **Readiness**: 4/5 (clearer framework than SEC for commodity-class assets)
- **Actions**: Register as FCM (Futures Commission Merchant) if offering leveraged crypto products; ensure no unregistered swaps

## IRS (Internal Revenue Service)
- **Framework**: Crypto treated as property for federal tax purposes; capital gains reporting required
- **Monero**: Same property treatment; privacy features do not exempt from tax reporting
- **RWA**: Tokenized assets subject to property taxation on disposition
- **Readiness**: 4/5 (clear guidance)
- **Actions**: Implement 1099-K/B reporting for marketplace transactions; ensure W-8/W-9 collection for international users

## FinCEN (Financial Crimes Enforcement Network)
- **Framework**: MSB (Money Services Business) registration required for crypto exchanges; Travel Rule for transactions >=$3,000
- **Monero**: Enhanced AML scrutiny for privacy coins; MSB registration required if operating as exchanger/admin
- **RWA**: Token transfer agents subject to AML/KYC under Bank Secrecy Act
- **Readiness**: 4/5 (established MSB framework)
- **Actions**: File MSB registration with FinCEN; implement Travel Rule compliance; designate AML compliance officer; conduct annual independent review

## Overall regulatory readiness

Aggregate score (average of 4 regulators): 3.75/5 -> 75/100 -> passes 60-point entry gate. The US is the largest agricultural robotics market with a workable, if complex, regulatory regime.
