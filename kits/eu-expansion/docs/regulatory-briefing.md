# Regulatory Briefing: EU Crypto/RWA Landscape (issue #17)

Per-country regulatory research for target markets. This is a static snapshot; operators must verify with live sources and legal counsel before operating.

## Italy (IT) -- Home Base
- **Regulator**: Banca d'Italia / CONSOB
- **Framework**: Virtual Asset Service Provider (VASP) registration under EU MiCAR (Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation, effective 2024)
- **Monero**: Privacy coins under MiCAR -- enhanced AML/KYC requirements but not banned
- **RWA tokenization**: Smart-contract based tokenization regulated under MiCAR Title III
- **Notes**: Home base advantage; existing Coldiretti partnership for agri sector

## Germany (DE)
- **Regulator**: BaFin
- **Framework**: KryptoZentVG (Crypto Custody Services Act) + MiCAR harmonization
- **Monero**: BaFin permits privacy-coin custody with enhanced AML controls
- **RWA**: BaFin permits electronic securities (eWpG) + tokenization under MiCAR
- **Notes**: NEURA Robotics HQ in Stuttgart; strong automatisierte agrar-robotik market

## United Kingdom (UK)
- **Regulator**: Financial Conduct Authority (FCA)
- **Framework**: Cryptoasset registration regime (post-Brexit, separate from MiCAR)
- **Monero**: FCA permits privacy coins for MLR-registered firms with Travel Rule compliance
- **Notes**: Small Robot Company (per-plant autonomous robotics); per-plant data model aligns with MyZubster's POI system

## France (FR)
- **Regulator**: AMF (Autorite des Marches Financiers) / Banque de France
- **Framework**: PACTE Act (2019) + MiCAR
- **Monero**: PACTE permits digital asset service providers; privacy coins subject to enhanced AML
- **Notes**: Paris Blockchain Week host city; FIMA adjacent agri events

## Spain (ES)
- **Regulator**: CNMV / Banco de Espana
- **Framework**: MiCAR transposition (2024-2025) + national AML directives
- **Monero**: Permitted with enhanced AML; growing local Monero community
- **Notes**: FIMA agri-tech fair (feb); strong agricultural sector (olive, citrus, greenhouse)

## Netherlands (NL)
- **Regulator**: DNB / AFM
- **Framework**: MiCAR + national AMLWA
- **Monero**: High crypto adoption rate; DNB requires VASP registration
- **Notes**: Wageningen UR (agricultural research); precision-agri greenhouse leadership
